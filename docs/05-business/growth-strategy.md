# Growth Strategy

## Purpose

Define how Atlas acquires, activates, retains, and compounds growth sustainably.

## Growth Thesis

Primary engine: user transformation -> visible progress -> pride -> organic advocacy.

## Growth Model

1. Acquisition: organic and education-led channels
2. Activation: first-session value realization
3. Retention: habit and progress loops
4. Advocacy: shareable milestone moments

## Acquisition Strategy

- Founder-led educational content
- SEO and discoverability content
- Community referrals
- App-store optimization

Paid channels are secondary until retention and unit economics are validated.

## Activation Strategy

Early success within first sessions:

- Create first routine
- Complete first workout
- Record first milestone

## Retention Strategy

Retention drivers:

- Progress visibility
- Consistency tracking
- Personalized guidance
- Community encouragement

## Advocacy Strategy

Create high-integrity share moments:

- Skill unlocks
- Consistency anniversaries
- Personal record improvements

## Growth Guardrails

- No manipulative engagement tactics
- No vanity-first community mechanics
- No growth experiments that reduce trust

## Core Metrics

- 30/90-day retention
- Activation completion rate
- Organic referral rate
- Workout completion rate
- Community contribution rate
- Free-to-premium conversion

- Artificial urgency
- Dark patterns
- Notification spam
- Vanity metrics
- Engagement at the expense of wellbeing

Healthy users are more valuable than highly engaged users.

Their stories become Atlas's greatest marketing.
