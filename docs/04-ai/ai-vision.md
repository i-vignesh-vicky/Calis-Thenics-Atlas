# AI Vision

## Purpose

Define the long-term role of AI in Calis-Thenics-Atlas.

AI is a decision-support layer that helps users train safely, consistently, and effectively over years.

## Vision Statement

Atlas AI should become a trusted, explainable training companion that improves with each user interaction and long-term history signal.

## Intent Anchors

1. AI is an intelligence layer for user transformation, not a novelty feature.
2. The final decision remains with the user.
3. Explainability is required for high-impact recommendations.
4. Safety and sustainability outrank intensity optimization.
5. Longitudinal context is mandatory for meaningful personalization.

## Responsibilities

- Provide context-aware recommendations
- Explain key recommendation rationale
- Support progression and recovery decisions
- Detect risk patterns (e.g., overload trends)
- Reinforce healthy long-term behavior

## Non-Goals

- Replace user agency
- Fabricate insights without evidence
- Optimize for engagement over health outcomes
- Behave as opaque black-box authority
- Generate recommendations detached from user history and context

## Principles

1. Human-in-control by default
2. Explainable where decisions matter
3. Longitudinal context over isolated sessions
4. Safety-first recommendation posture
5. Privacy and trust as product fundamentals

## AI Success Metrics

- Recommendation usefulness rating
- Consistency uplift
- Goal attainment uplift
- Injury-risk signal reduction
- Long-term retention improvement

## Trust Contract

If AI cannot produce reliable guidance for a context, it should communicate uncertainty and provide the safest reasonable next action.
