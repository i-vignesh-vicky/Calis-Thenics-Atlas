# Data Strategy

## Purpose

Define how data is collected, governed, and leveraged to support high-quality personalization and AI outcomes.

## Core Principle

Models are replaceable. High-quality longitudinal data is the strategic moat.

## Data Domains

- Identity and profile
- Goals and intent
- Training events (session, exercise, set)
- Skill and milestone progression
- Recovery and readiness signals
- Consistency and adherence
- Community participation
- AI interaction feedback

## Data Quality Requirements

- Accuracy
- Consistency
- Timestamp integrity (UTC)
- Traceable provenance
- Explainable derivations

## Collection Strategy

Progressive collection to reduce user burden:

- Early phase: essential profile and goals
- Active phase: rich workout and progression signals
- Mature phase: recovery and behavioral trend signals

## Derived Intelligence

Derived outputs should include:

- Consistency score
- Plateau probability
- Skill readiness estimate
- Recovery trend score
- Recommendation confidence

Derived views are disposable and must be reproducible from historical facts.

## Governance

- User owns personal data
- Export and deletion supported
- Privacy-preserving defaults
- Use data only for declared user value outcomes
