# Personalization

## Purpose

Define how Atlas adapts product experience and guidance to each user over time.

## Personalization Loop

Observe -> Understand -> Recommend -> Learn -> Refine

## Input Signals

- Goals and goal changes
- Experience level
- Workout history and adherence
- Skill progression velocity
- Recovery and fatigue indicators
- Preferences (time, equipment, exercise affinity)
- Behavioral patterns (skips, drop-offs, completion quality)

## Personalization Surfaces

- Daily training suggestions
- Progress summaries
- Recovery-aware intensity guidance
- Exercise substitutions
- Motivation and reflection prompts

## Design Rules

- Prefer invisible personalization over settings overload
- Adapt conservatively when confidence is low
- Let users provide explicit feedback to tune outputs
- Keep recommendations aligned with safety and sustainability

## Maturity Timeline

- Day 1: Profile-driven defaults
- Month 1: Preference and adherence adaptation
- Year 1: Recovery and progression-pattern adaptation
- Multi-year: Longitudinal coaching style adaptation
