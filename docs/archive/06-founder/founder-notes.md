# Founder Notes

## Purpose

Capture founder intent, leadership standards, and cultural constraints that should remain stable as the company scales.

## Founder Intent

Atlas exists to solve a lived problem: fragmented fitness guidance and inconsistent long-term progression support.

## Mission Reminder

Build a platform that helps users improve health, capability, and discipline over years, not weeks.

## Leadership Standards

- Mission-first decisions
- Evidence-informed prioritization
- Long-term system thinking
- Craft quality and accountability

## Team Expectations

- Own outcomes, not only tasks
- Communicate transparently
- Improve continuously
- Protect user trust in every decision

## Culture Guardrails

Atlas should not become:

- A feature factory
- A vanity metric machine
- An engagement-maximization product at the expense of outcomes

## Founder Principle

Build for decades, not demos.
