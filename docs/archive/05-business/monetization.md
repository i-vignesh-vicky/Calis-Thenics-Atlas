# Monetization Strategy

## Purpose

Define a sustainable revenue model that preserves user trust and aligns with product mission.

## Monetization Principle

Users should pay because Atlas creates meaningful incremental value, not because essentials are artificially withheld.

## Model

Freemium with clear value separation:

- Free: habit-building and core progress workflows
- Premium: advanced intelligence and optimization workflows

## Free Tier (Core)

- Workout logging and history
- Routine creation
- Program participation
- Skill and milestone tracking
- Progress timeline and consistency tracking
- Basic community participation

## Premium Tier (Advanced)

- Advanced analytics and reports
- AI coaching and adaptive guidance
- Recovery intelligence and personalization depth
- Wearable and advanced integrations

## Non-Negotiable Free Access

Never monetize:

- Access to user-owned data
- Data export and deletion controls
- Basic fitness tracking and habit loop functionality

## Pricing Principles

- Simple and transparent
- No dark patterns
- No hidden fees
- Clear outcome-driven value narrative

## Revenue Expansion (Future)

- Coach marketplace
- Premium expert programs
- Team and club plans
- Enterprise wellness offerings

## Business Metrics

- Free-to-premium conversion
- Churn and retention
- MRR/ARR
- Subscription lifetime
- Premium feature adoption

These require continuous infrastructure, AI costs, and engineering investment. Users are much more likely to perceive them as fair reasons to subscribe.
