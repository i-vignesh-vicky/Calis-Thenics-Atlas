# Problem Statement

## Purpose

This document defines the core problem Calis-Thenics-Atlas solves, who experiences it, why existing solutions are insufficient, and what outcomes the product must deliver.

## Problem Summary

People want to become healthier, stronger, and more consistent, but most fitness products fail to support long-term transformation. Users either abandon generic plans, get lost in fragmented tooling, or train without feedback loops that support progress and recovery.

## Primary Problem

Users cannot sustain meaningful fitness progress over years because they lack a single system that combines:

- Structured training guidance
- Flexible routine customization
- Longitudinal progress tracking
- Recovery-aware recommendations
- Community accountability
- Personalized coaching over time

## Why Existing Solutions Fall Short

Current products often optimize one dimension only:

- Tracking apps: Good logging, weak coaching
- Program apps: Good plans, weak adaptation
- Social apps: High engagement, weak progression quality
- Wearable apps: Good recovery signals, weak training execution

Users are forced to switch between multiple tools, creating fragmented data and inconsistent behavior.

## User Pain Points

- "I do not know what to do next."
- "I cannot see if I am actually improving."
- "I keep starting and stopping routines."
- "I do not know if I should train hard or recover today."
- "I lose motivation when progress feels invisible."
- "I use several apps, but none understand my full journey."

## Product Opportunity

Calis-Thenics-Atlas can become the lifelong fitness companion that unifies training execution, progress intelligence, and personalized guidance.

## Desired Outcomes

The product must help users:

- Train consistently
- Improve safely
- Understand progress with clarity
- Receive recommendations grounded in their context
- Build identity-level habits

## Scope of This Problem (MVP)

In MVP, focus on:

- Workout system (programs + custom routines)
- Logging and history
- Skill and milestone progression
- Basic progress analytics
- Consistency and streak support
- Lightweight community accountability

Deferred for later phases:

- Advanced AI coaching
- Full wearable-driven recovery intelligence
- Nutrition intelligence
