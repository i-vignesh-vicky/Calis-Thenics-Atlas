# Introduction

Fitness is not a single market.

A beginner trying to lose weight has fundamentally different needs from an advanced calisthenics athlete preparing for a planche.

Instead of treating all users the same, our platform recognizes that every individual is on a unique fitness journey.

Our goal is to meet users where they are today while growing with them for years.

---

# Product Strategy

Rather than optimizing for one niche forever, our strategy is progressive expansion.

## Phase 1

Primary focus:

Calisthenics

Reason:

This is the founder's domain expertise.

It allows us to deeply understand one community before expanding.

---

## Phase 2

Expand into:

• Strength Training
• Gym Programming
• Hybrid Athletes

---

## Phase 3

Expand into:

• Running
• Cardio
• Functional Fitness
• Mobility
• Yoga

---

## Phase 4

Become a complete fitness ecosystem.

At this stage, fitness modality becomes secondary.

The platform focuses on helping every user improve regardless of how they train.

---

# Core User

## The Self-Improver

This is the person we are designing the platform around.

Characteristics:

• Wants to become healthier
• Wants measurable progress
• Values learning
• Is willing to stay consistent
• Believes fitness is a long-term investment

They are not looking for shortcuts.

They want sustainable progress.

---

## Goals

• Become stronger

• Build muscle

• Learn new skills

• Improve endurance

• Improve mobility

• Lose fat

• Gain confidence

• Build discipline

• Stay healthy for life

---

## Pain Points

They often experience:

• Information overload

• Confusing workout advice

• Lack of consistency

• Plateauing

• Injury

• Poor recovery

• Difficulty tracking progress

• Motivation loss

• Difficulty knowing what to do next

---

## What They Need

They need:

• Structure

• Flexibility

• Accountability

• Encouragement

• Clear progress

• Personalization

• Community

• Education

# Primary User Segments

---

# 1. Beginners

Experience:

0–6 months

Typical thoughts:

"I don't know where to start."

"I can't even do one pull-up."

"I don't want to look foolish."

Needs:

• Guided onboarding

• Simple workouts

• Proper exercise demonstrations

• Positive reinforcement

• Small achievable milestones

Success looks like:

• Completing first workout

• First push-up

• First pull-up

• Building consistency

---

# 2. Intermediate Athletes

Experience:

6 months–5 years

This is expected to become our largest early audience.

Typical thoughts:

"I've stopped making progress."

"I've tried many apps."

"I keep changing routines."

"I don't know if my programming is good."

Needs:

• Better programming

• Progress tracking

• Skill progressions

• Recovery awareness

• Performance analytics

• Personal records

Success looks like:

Steady long-term improvement without injury.

---

# 3. Advanced Athletes

Experience:

5+ years

Goals:

• Optimize performance

• Break plateaus

• Improve efficiency

• Fine tune programming

• Advanced analytics

Needs:

• Detailed tracking

• Volume analysis

• Recovery insights

• Personalized recommendations

• Historical trends

Success looks like:

Continuous optimization rather than dramatic improvement.

Beginner

↓

Learns proper movement

Achieves first milestones

↓

Starts tracking progress

↓

↓

↓

Improves recovery

# What Success Looks Like

A user who has been on the platform for two years should say:

"I've become stronger than I ever imagined."

"I rarely get injured."

"I understand my own body."

"I no longer guess what to do."

"I've built habits that changed my life."

"I've met people who genuinely support me."

"I've helped others begin their own journey."

At this point, the platform has become more than software.

It has become part of the user's identity.

---

# Users We Are NOT Optimizing For

At least in the early stages, we are not building primarily for:

• Professional bodybuilders

• Elite powerlifters

• Competitive CrossFit specialists

• Users looking only for quick weight loss

• People seeking crash diets

• Users who expect instant results with minimal effort

These users may still benefit from the platform, but they are not our primary design audience.

---

# Guiding Principle

Every product decision should begin with one question:

"Will this genuinely help someone become healthier, stronger, more consistent, and more confident over the long term?"

If the answer is no, the feature should be reconsidered.

### Architect's Note

One insight that emerged from our discussions is that **your real target user is not "a calisthenics athlete."**

It's **a person committed to self-improvement through fitness.**

Calisthenics is simply the entry point because it is your expertise and gives the product an authentic starting community. Over time, the platform should become modality-agnostic while preserving the same philosophy.

The next document should be **`product-philosophy.md`**. This will define the beliefs that every product and engineering decision must uphold. It will become one of the most referenced documents in the entire repository.
