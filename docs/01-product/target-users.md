# Target Users

## Purpose

This document defines who Calis-Thenics-Atlas is built for, what jobs users hire the product to do, and which segments are prioritized in each stage.

## User Strategy

The product is built for people committed to self-improvement through fitness. Calisthenics is the entry focus; the long-term strategy is modality expansion without losing the same philosophy.

## Primary User

### The Self-Improver

Characteristics:

- Wants measurable progress
- Values consistency over shortcuts
- Willing to learn and adapt
- Seeks long-term health and capability

Needs:

- Clear structure with flexibility
- Reliable progress signals
- Personalized guidance
- Encouraging accountability

## Primary Segments

### 1. Beginners (0-6 months)

Needs:

- Guided onboarding
- Simple plans
- Early milestone reinforcement

### 2. Intermediate Athletes (6 months-5 years)

Needs:

- Better progression logic
- Strong history and analytics
- Recovery-aware recommendations

### 3. Advanced Athletes (5+ years)

Needs:

- High-fidelity tracking
- Deep trend analysis
- Precision personalization

## Secondary Segments

- Coaches (future)
- Creators and mentors
- Casual health users

## Jobs To Be Done

### Functional Jobs

- Help me know what to train today
- Help me track what I did accurately
- Help me understand if I am improving
- Help me train consistently

### Emotional Jobs

- Help me feel capable and confident
- Help me feel supported, not judged
- Help me maintain motivation through reflection

### Social Jobs

- Help me share meaningful progress
- Help me learn from others
- Help me belong to a healthy community

## Users We Are Not Optimizing For (MVP)

- Short-term crash-transformation seekers
- Users expecting results with minimal effort
- Highly specialized elite sport workflows as first priority
