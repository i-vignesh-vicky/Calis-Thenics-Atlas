# Epic 13 — Release and Launch

## Purpose

Prepare and ship the MVP. Covers beta build, staging, smoke tests, feedback collection, production readiness, and launch stabilization.

This epic consolidates three delivery phases that share no product capability boundary: beta preparation, production readiness, and launch operations.

## Functional Requirements Satisfied

None from the portfolio FR list. Release is a delivery operation, not a product capability.
