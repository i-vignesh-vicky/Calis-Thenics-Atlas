# STORY-066 · Prepare Launch Assets and Support Links

Epic: [epic-13-release-and-launch](../epics/epic-13-release-and-launch.md)
Estimate: 3 hrs
Priority: High
Status: Todo

## Objective

Prepare the external-facing assets and support resources needed for the public launch.

## Implementation Steps

- [ ] Write or collect the app store or distribution metadata: name, description, screenshots, and category.
- [ ] Publish a support contact or FAQ page that users can reach from within the app.
- [ ] Add a privacy policy and terms of service link that is accessible before account creation.

## Technical Notes

- Support links must be live before the app is distributed; a broken link on launch day is a trust signal failure.
- Screenshots should reflect the actual shipped UI, not mockups.

## Tests

- [ ] Verify all support and policy links resolve correctly.
- [ ] Verify support links are reachable from within the app.

## Documentation

- [ ] Document the launch assets location and how to update them.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] App store or distribution metadata is ready.
- [ ] Support links are live and reachable from the app.
- [ ] Privacy policy and terms of service links are accessible.

## Deliverables

- Launch metadata
- Support contact or FAQ page
- Privacy policy and terms of service links
