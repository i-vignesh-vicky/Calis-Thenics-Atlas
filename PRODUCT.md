# Product

This document is the short entrypoint for the product vision, target users, MVP scope, and roadmap.

The detailed product guidance lives in [docs/01-product](docs/01-product).

## Core Belief

Calis-Thenics-Atlas is not a workout tracker. It is a long-term companion that helps users become healthier, stronger, more disciplined, and more capable.

## MVP Scope (v1.0)

All 11 features ship in MVP:

1. Identity and Access
2. User Profile and Goals
3. Exercise Library
4. Programs
5. Routine Builder
6. Workout Execution and Logging
7. Skills and Milestones
8. Progress and Consistency
9. Community and Challenges
10. Notifications and Search
11. AI and Personalization (basic recommendations only)

## Explicitly Deferred (v2.0+)

- Advanced AI coaching
- Social login (OAuth)
- Full nutrition intelligence
- Wearable-native recovery intelligence
- Trainer marketplace
- Direct messaging

## Decision Filter

Before shipping any feature, ask:

1. Does this help long-term healthy behavior?
2. Does this reduce friction and ambiguity?
3. Does this reinforce genuine progress over vanity?
4. Does this respect user attention?

## Focus

- Product philosophy and intent
- Target users and their needs
- MVP scope and functional requirements
- Delivery roadmap and sequencing
